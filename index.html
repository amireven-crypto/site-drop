Redesign concept · Barbershop · Vienna

The Barber Crew

A modern redesign of a barbershop website in Vienna’s 2nd district. A deep-blue look with the full price list, online booking through Treatwell, a tap-to-call phone number and one-tap directions in Google Maps and Apple Maps.

  • Redesign
  • Mobile-first
  • Online booking
  • Google & Apple Maps
  • Tap to call

E-commerce · Demo store

FreshDrop Shop

A complete online store built from scratch: products in categories like home, kitchen, pets, tools and car, product pages with options, bundles, a working cart with a free-shipping bar, and page templates for the legal info an EU shop needs (shipping, returns, privacy, terms).

  • Online store
  • Product pages
  • Cart & bundles
  • Mobile-friendly
  • Dark mode

IKG Wien

A concept redesign of the Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien website: multilingual (German, English, Hebrew and Russian), senior-friendly typography and controls, and an accessible, mobile-first layout with a clear language switcher and readable contrast.
