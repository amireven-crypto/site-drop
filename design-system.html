Design system

The mechanisms, in PIXELPOP color.

Layout, type, glass, the light stream, the loader ring, the logo plate and the scope meter follow the public Relay reference. The palette does not. Every color below is taken from the previous PIXELPOP stylesheet. Relay’s lime is not a token here.

Color

Raw swatches stay the same in light and dark. Semantic tokens (--bg, --fg, --muted, glass) switch with prefers-color-scheme. Small text on blue uses --blue-link (#1A6AE8) or --blue-text (#1559C7), not white on #2B7BFF.

Dark scheme lifts the small-text candy colors: pink #FF7AB8, blue #8DB9FF, mint #4FEBD2, orange #FFB066, on #050507. Night chapter grounds from the previous site, #0b0a16 and #100e1e, stay available as --night and --night-soft.

Type

Geist for interface and display. Instrument Serif italic for the one stressed phrase in a headline — the job lime italics did on the reference, set in PIXELPOP pink-text. Both faces are self-hosted (SIL OFL).

Glass and shadow

Glass is the hero window and the floating panels: a translucent fill, a 1px light edge, an inset highlight and a soft offset shadow. Blur is 18px (12px on phones) and turns off for prefers-reduced-transparency.

This line arrives out of focus and settles.

  • Loader: a candy ring counts 0–100, then the mark translates and scales into the nav logo.
  • Stream: one canvas ribbon in blue, mint, orange, pink and violet, the bright knot following scroll. Paused when the tab is hidden. A single still frame when motion is reduced.
  • Plate: the pink field scales out of the top of its section (toward the logo) and folds back as you pass it. Ten steps, no inline styles, so the content-security policy can stay strict.
  • Sections with .settle blur in, then clear, once.
  • prefers-reduced-motion skips the loader, the tilt, the plate scale and the blur.
