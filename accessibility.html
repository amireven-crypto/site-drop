Accessibility

Accessibility statement

PIXELPOP wants everyone to be able to use this website – including people who use a screen reader, the keyboard only, magnification or other assistive technology. This statement applies to amireven-crypto.github.io/site-drop.

Standard and legal basis

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This includes the WCAG 2.1 AA requirements of the European standard EN 301 549, which is the reference for the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and the Austrian Accessibility Act (Barrierefreiheitsgesetz, BaFG).

For visitors in Israel, the site also follows Israeli Standard IS 5568 (based on WCAG, level AA) under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

Conformance status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 level AA: most content meets the requirements; the exceptions are listed under “Known limitations”.

Assessment method: self-assessment on 18 November 2026 with automated checks (axe-core, Lighthouse) and manual tests – keyboard only, screen widths from 360 px, 200 % zoom, Chrome and Safari (WebKit). The site has not yet been audited by an independent accessibility expert.

What we have done

  • A “Skip to content” link, a clear heading structure and page regions (header, main content, footer).
  • All functions work with the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • Text and controls meet the WCAG contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and interface elements).
  • Images have text alternatives; purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The layout works on phones from 360 px wide and with 200 % zoom without sideways scrolling; touch targets are at least 24 × 24 px, most are 44 × 44 px.
  • Animations are reduced when your device is set to “reduce motion”, and they can be stopped in the accessibility menu.
  • Each language version sets the correct page language and reading direction (right to left for Hebrew).

Accessibility menu

The round button with the accessibility symbol at the bottom of the screen opens a menu on every page. It works with the mouse, touch and keyboard, and the Esc key closes it. You can enlarge the text in three steps up to 150 %, and switch on high contrast, grayscale, highlighted links, a readable font, wider text spacing, stopped animations, a big cursor and a highlighted focus. The settings are saved only in your browser (local storage), are not sent to us and can be reset at any time.

The menu is an extra. The site itself is built to be accessible without it, and the settings of your browser and operating system (zoom, reduce motion, high contrast) keep working.

Known limitations

  • The project pictures are screenshots of other websites; their text alternative names the project but does not describe every detail.
  • The contact form opens your own e-mail program; it does not send anything by itself.
  • On large screens a decorative light animation runs in the background. It is hidden from screen readers and stops with “Stop animations” or your device’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Linked third-party websites and services are not covered by this statement.
