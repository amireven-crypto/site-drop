Privacy

Privacy policy

In short: this website uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. We only receive personal data if you write to us by email.

1. Who is responsible

PIXELPOP, Vienna, Austria. Name and address of the owner: see the legal notice (Impressum) once it is published.

Contact for all privacy questions: pixelpopsite@gmail.com

2. Visiting the website (hosting)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub automatically processes your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser and device type (server logs). We have no access to these logs.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because GitHub is based in the USA, data may be transferred there; GitHub states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR).

3. Contacting us by email

The contact form does not send anything by itself: it only opens your own email program with your text. Nothing you type is stored on this website or sent to anyone else.

If you send us an email, we receive your email address, your name if you give it and the content of your message. We use them only to answer you and, if you order a website, to prepare and carry out the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes the messages for us.

We delete enquiries that do not lead to a contract no later than 12 months after the last message. If a contract is concluded, business records are kept for 7 years as required by Austrian tax law (§ 132 BAO).

4. No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media tools. All fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other third party.

Your browser only remembers the language you chose and your accessibility settings (local storage). These stay on your device, are never sent to us and are strictly necessary for the function you asked for (§ 165(3) TKG 2021). You can delete them at any time in your browser settings or with “Reset” in the accessibility menu.

Links to other websites (for example our project examples) only open those sites when you click them; their own privacy policies apply there.

5. Security

The site is only delivered over encrypted HTTPS. A strict Content Security Policy allows scripts, styles and fonts only from this website, and no forms send data to a server.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just write to pixelpopsite@gmail.com; we reply within one month. Providing data is voluntary, and we do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

You can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at. Visitors in Israel can also contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.
