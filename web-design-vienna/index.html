PIXELPOP · Vienna

Web design in Vienna that works on a phone and a desktop

PIXELPOP is a web design studio for businesses in Vienna and beyond. The work covers new websites, redesigns and online stores, with booking, maps and tap-to-call when the business needs them. You review a live preview on your own phone and computer before anything goes public.

Start a project → pixelpopsite@gmail.com

A studio that shows the work live

If you search for a web designer in Vienna, you should be able to open real pages, not only a mood board. PIXELPOP publishes live demos. The Barber Crew is a redesign concept for a barbershop in Vienna’s 2nd district, with a price list, Treatwell booking, tap-to-call and directions in Google Maps and Apple Maps. FreshDrop Shop is a demo store with product pages, a cart and EU legal page templates, and no real orders or payments. IKG Wien is a concept redesign of a multilingual community site, clearly labelled as a concept, not an official site.

There is no rate card and no invented testimonials on this website. Scope is quoted after you have seen a preview. Contact is by email only, at pixelpopsite@gmail.com.

English, German, Hebrew and Russian

The PIXELPOP site itself, and several of the demos, can be read in more than one language. If your business in Vienna serves visitors or residents who switch between languages, that can be built into the site from the start, with a language switch that changes the text and leaves the layout alone.
